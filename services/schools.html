Schools Photography

Class portraits, prospectus shoots, life-on-campus imagery and event coverage.

About this service

Bespoke schools photography in London

We work with nurseries, primary schools, secondary schools and independent schools across London — providing photography that is warm, considered and genuinely on-brand for each setting.

Whether you need annual class & individual portraits, a full prospectus refresh, an open day documented or sports-day coverage, our team handles the project end-to-end with full DBS-checked photographers.

We supply password-protected parent ordering portals, integrated with print fulfilment, so families can order directly without administrative load on the school.

What's included

  • Annual individual & class portraits
  • Prospectus & marketing imagery
  • Sports day, performances & events
  • Parent online ordering portal
  • DBS-checked photographers
  • Full GDPR & safeguarding compliance
